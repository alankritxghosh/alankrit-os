## Collaboration model (as practised)

He is the router and judge across several agents. Claude builds; Codex reviews (or builds when Claude is out of credits); he pastes reviews between them and decides on short options. The agents carry method, memory and documentation. He carries direction, taste, the public voice and the final call. When trust breaks, he asks for provenance rather than taking the work back.
