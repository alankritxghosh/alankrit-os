## LinkedIn

LinkedIn-1 (story first)
From idea to launch on Product Hunt, Icarus took me 60 days.
I'm a finance student, not an engineer.

For marketing and distribution I kept asking what an agent could do instead of me, because a lot of those tasks looked trivial. That got me to a marketing system much earlier than I would have had otherwise.

Then I went looking for what the research says about this.
A study at IG Group had 12 web analysts, 26 marketing specialists and 40 technology specialists write investing articles with AI. The marketers scored 3.92 out of 5 against 3.96 for the analysts. The technology group scored 3.42.

The closer you are to the task, the more AI helps you.

I wrote up what that means for generalists, including the study that worries me most: BCG consultants using AI were 19 percentage points less likely to be right on a task outside what the model could do.

Where does your edge sit?

LinkedIn-2 (the weekend)
Moving Icarus from keyword search to semantic retrieval took one weekend, Saturday and Sunday.
It broke the whole way through.
The worst of it was a timeout bug in the cloud that ran for 30 hours. My agents and I fixed it and worked out why it happened.

Engineering isn't my lane. I knew what I wanted the system to be and I could tell when it wasn't that.

That weekend is where I found out how far my edge goes.

How do you find yours?
