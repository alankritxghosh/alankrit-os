# Role-model scouting, pass 2 (deeper)

Date: 2026-10-04. Same ten accounts as pass 1 (`2026-10-04-role-model-report.md`). Read-only: logged-in page views plus public web search; nothing posted, liked, followed or sent. This pass adds: how each account grew (from public sources), their replies and comments on **other people's** posts, and engagement measured against follower count.

## Limits (read first)

- **Samples are small, set by the sites.** X only keeps a handful of posts in the page at once: 5-7 original posts and 4-20 replies per account. LinkedIn showed 5 recent items per account. Everything below is a snapshot of roughly 2 days (3 for Tony Dinh), not a trend.
- **Growth evidence is secondary.** The sites do not show follower history. Figures come from the accounts' own posts or from third-party pages, graded below. Absence of evidence on paid growth or engagement pods is **not** evidence they did not happen.
- **Engagement ratios are crude.** X: median views of recent original posts divided by current followers. LinkedIn: median reactions divided by current followers. Different platforms, different meanings.
- **Whose words:** the replies quoted are the role models' public posts, quoted in fragments for analysis only.

## 5. What this changes

1. **The "organic" test is only partly passed.** Marc Lou's documented 8-months-to-1,000 start is the strongest evidence. Most others are unverified or sell growth services.
2. **Best fits for your stated style (funny, personal, builder, swearing on X):** Marc Lou (shipping in public, warm, short), Dillon Mulroy (lowercase, personal, day job), Amy Watts (humour, highest LinkedIn ratio, comments playfully on others), Tony Dinh (shows his work).
3. **Weak fits:** Michel Lieben, Richard Goold and Justin Welsh are service or newsletter businesses; Lara Acosta sells personal-branding growth. Useful as a contrast, not as models for your voice.
4. **Two practical findings for you:** (a) answer every comment on your own posts, quickly, by name, the one habit all ten share; (b) a reply that adds a specific number, story or list beats a one-word reply from the same person.
5. **Gap:** every account is far bigger than you. Nothing here shows what works between 30 and 1,000 followers.

## Recommended list (needs your approval)

- **X (5):** keep Marc Lou, Tony Dinh, Dillon Mulroy. Replace Pieter Levels and Arvid Kahl, whose growth is unverified and whose scale is far from yours, with two builders in the 1K-20K range who grew in the last year.
- **LinkedIn (5):** keep Amy Watts and Lara Acosta (for the comment-on-others behaviour). Replace Lieben, Goold and Welsh with builders or explorers closer to your lane who grew from a small base.
